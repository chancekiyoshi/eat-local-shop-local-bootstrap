Eat Local, Shop Local

Support O‘ahu’s local businesses, artisans, and farmers.

Neighborhoods

Explore Chinatown, Wahiawā, Kaimukī, Kapahulu, and Hale‘iwa for local boutiques, bakeries, and iconic plate lunches.

Farmers’ Markets

Enjoy fresh fruits, local seafood, island coffee, and artisanal confections at markets hosted around O‘ahu weekly.

Mementos of Aloha

Shop made-in-Hawai‘i art, books, apparel, and fresh cookies from local makers like Nā Mea Hawai‘i and Honolulu Cookie Company.
